    Where do I get my friend file?

    In the friend.com app: go back to your list of friends, tap the download button (the square with an arrow) at the top, then tap the friend you want. When the row says “<name> friend file”, tap Download.

    On the website: open your list of friends, click Download a friend to a file (the arrow icon) at the top, click the friend, then click Download when it’s ready.

    You get friend_<name>.zip. Import it here as it is. A .friendlocal file from friend-local on another device imports the same way.
